# 06 · What the user likes (and doesn't)

Collected from feedback so far. Add to it every time.

## Loves

- **Favourite episodes:** The Gap, Rain Check, Yes, Last Minute, Perfect, Seen, What If, Battery.
- **What those share:** one clear visual metaphor, a problem anyone recognises, escalation, a warm ending, a short quote.
- **Relatable, common topics** anyone "gets" automatically, without explanation. Keep it general, not niche.
- **Curiosity first:** open on something that makes you wonder what Wobbin is doing, then reveal it.
- **Time-of-day changes:** evening and night scenes; colours shifting through a story.
- **Background elements** that fit the story (a house, a tree, a bus stop).
- **Smooth motion** and good animation principles (anticipation, ease in and out, squash and stretch).
- **8-bit colours**, good colours generally; the site's blue `#3B7DD8` is a favourite.
- **Soft, pleasing sound effects.** No music, no noise.
- **Seamless loops** where each loop feels more intense or satisfying.
- **Being creative:** new characters, new objects, new ideas.

## Doesn't like

- Anything that feels **"AI-ish" or programmed**: mechanical timing, identical repeats, robotic sounds. Hand-made warmth matters.
- **Loud or harsh sound.** The caption voice and footsteps should be very faint; everything a bit faint.
- **Big steps:** Wobbin's stride in the site's band was too long (now capped).
- **Things looking too big or zoomed in** (the website's scale was reduced).
- **Pinned scroll-scrub sections** on the website.
- The font Bricolage Grotesque (now Clash Display + Satoshi on the site).

## How they like to work

- Decisive: "do the best you can do, don't ask me questions" during long runs (often overnight).
- Wants a push to the live site when something is finished, and asks for it in chat.
- Rendering every MP4 takes too long; skip bulk renders unless asked. Single downloads from the site player are fine.
- No `Co-Authored-By: Claude` lines in commits.
- Wobbin is referred to without gendered pronouns in captions and copy.
