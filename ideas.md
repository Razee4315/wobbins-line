# Line Shorts — idea bank

Everyday struggles people instantly recognise, each told with one character (Bo), one
line and a few pixel colours. Each idea is written as a visual metaphor, never literally
("assignment", "exam", "boss").

## Season 3 (built)

| Short | The struggle | The metaphor | Closing line |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Last Minute** | Putting things off for days, then doing a week's work in one night when the deadline hits | A pile of blocks and a sign counting down the days; Bo naps, plays, cloud-watches as the days fly by, then builds the whole tower in a frantic final night | "why is the last night always the most productive?" |
| **Yes** | People-pleasing: never saying no until you're crushed | Friends keep stacking boxes on Bo's head; Bo's own little flower gets buried; the stack collapses; Bo finally says no and nobody minds | "you can say no. they'll be okay." |
| **Perfect** | Perfectionism: redoing work forever over one tiny flaw | Bo paints a pixel heart, spots one wrong pixel, wipes it all, again and again, while a friend happily finishes painting after painting | "done is better than perfect." |
| **Battery** | The social battery: fun, but draining | A pixel battery over Bo's head drains bar by bar at a party; Bo slips away to a quiet tree and recharges; two friends come and sit with Bo | "it's okay to need quiet. real friends get it." |
| **Seen** | Waiting for a reply and spiralling | Bo sends a paper plane; "seen"; typing dots appear and vanish; the worry scribble grows; the plane comes back with a heart | "not every silence is about you." |

## Season 6 · little things (built)

Every one opens on a small mystery (what is Bo doing?), then reveals it, escalates, turns, and ends on the quote.

| Short | The struggle | The metaphor | Closing line |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The Other Line** | Impatience | Two ice-cream lines; whichever one Bo picks stops moving. Bo sits, waits, watches the light change, and gets the biggest cone | "the other line always looks faster. yours is moving too." |
| **Why Was I Here?** | Forgetting | A thought bubble (water the plant) pops every time Bo walks through the doorway, even tiptoeing; so the plant rides along on Bo's head | "it's not just you. doorways really do make you forget." |
| **Lost** | Losing things | Frantic search: cushions, drawer, rug, plant, a racing clock. The keys hang off Bo's tuft from the first frame | "slow down. it's usually closer than you think." |

## Season 8 · daily routine (built 2026-10-30)

Picked from research on everyday "microstressors" (small daily frustrations people say wear them down
more than big ones): the clothes chair, the two-minute task put off for weeks, checking the lock, forgetting
to drink water, walking behind someone slow.

| Short | The struggle | The metaphor | Closing line |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The Chair** | Clothes on the chair instead of the wardrobe | Each day's clean laundry lands on the chair ("later."); by day four the pile is taller than Bo and blinks; one sock costs Bo an avalanche | "put it away now. future you says thanks." |
| **Two Minutes** | The tiny task you dread for weeks | A flickering lamp, new bulbs right beside it, and an ink cloud of dread that grows each day; the fix: a stopwatch reading 0:02 | "it took two seconds. the dread took two weeks." |
| **Did I Lock It?** | Going back to check | A red elastic of worry from the door to Bo's tuft yanks Bo home; a slow, deliberate lock and a gold star sticker let it go | "you checked. you can let it go." |
| **Water Yourself** | Looking after everything but yourself | Bo waters a plant daily while Bo's own glass waits; the plant blooms, Bo wilts; the plant flips the glass to Bo | "you need watering too." |
| **Slow Walker** | Stuck behind someone slow | One line means no overtaking; Bo stops pushing, looks where they look, and the slow lane has the sunset | "some days, the slow lane has the best view." |

## Backlog (fresh ideas)

- **Burnout** — Bo is a candle-flame keeper, lighting everyone else's lanterns until Bo's own flame is a flicker; Bo finally lights its own first. *"you can't pour from an empty cup."*
- **Gratitude** — Bo walks past small good things without seeing them (a flower, a sunbeam, a friend's wave); the second time round Bo collects each one into a growing pixel bouquet. *"look how much you already have."*
- **Restart** — Bo's tower falls; Bo stares at the mess, then builds something different (and better) from the same blocks. *"starting over isn't starting from zero."*

### Next ideas (added 2026-10-26). Each has a curiosity hook for the first seconds.

- **Sorry** — *Hook:* Bo bows to a lamp post. *Reveal:* Bo says sorry to everything (a door, a cat, the rain), and the little "sorry" bubbles pile up and weigh Bo down; a friend says "you didn't do anything wrong." *"save your sorry for when you mean it."*
- **Group Chat** — *Hook:* a phone-shaped box on the line keeps jumping and buzzing. *Reveal:* 99+ messages pour out as pixel speech bubbles burying Bo; Bo mutes it and goes outside with one friend. *"you don't have to answer everything right now."*
- **On Hold** — *Hook:* Bo stands frozen, swaying to a tiny tune. *Reveal:* Bo's on hold; the hold music loops and the day goes from morning to night; finally Bo hangs up and walks to the shop, and it's done in a second. *"some things are faster if you just go."*
- **Blank Page** — *Hook:* Bo circles a big white square, terrified. *Reveal:* a blank page; every first mark looks wrong, until Bo just scribbles, and the scribble becomes a drawing. *"the first line is allowed to be bad."*
- **Clap Back** — *Hook:* everyone's clapping; Bo's arms... Bo has no arms. *Reveal:* a friend wins a prize; Bo feels jealous (a green cloud), then cheers loudest, and the friend shares the crown. *"cheering for others doesn't make you smaller."*
- **Water Yourself** — *Hook:* Bo waters a plant every morning, very proudly. *Reveal:* the plant thrives while Bo droops more each day; the plant "waters" Bo back. *"you need looking after too."*
- **Offline** — *Hook:* Bo holds a wifi signal like a balloon. *Reveal:* the signal pops; panic, then Bo notices the sunset, a bird, a friend. When the wifi returns, Bo ignores it. *"the world still works offline."*
- **Homesick** — *Hook:* Bo walks with a tiny house floating above like a thought bubble. *Reveal:* new city, new everything; the little house shrinks as new friends and places fill the bubble, but it never disappears. *"home is something you carry."*
- **Small Wins** — *Hook:* Bo does a victory dance over... a made bed. *Reveal:* Bo celebrates every tiny thing (socks matched, plant watered) with confetti, and by evening the line is full of colour. *"celebrate the small wins. they're still wins."*
- **Goodbye** — *Hook:* two friends walking in perfect step. *Reveal:* one is moving away; the line splits into two lines that drift apart, but a string of little hearts stays between them. *"distance changes the line, not the friendship."*
- **Midnight Snack** — *Hook:* a fridge light in the dark, and two eyes. *Reveal:* Bo tiptoes for "one" snack and returns again and again; a gentle, funny one about habits. *"be kind to yourself, even at midnight."*
- **The Mirror** — *Hook:* Bo stares into a mirror and pulls the body in, pushes the tuft down. *Reveal:* the mirror shows a wobbly, unkind version; friends see the real Bo, and the mirror slowly agrees. *"you look like someone people love."*
- **Burnout, gently** — *Hook:* Bo lights lanterns for everyone down the line. *Reveal:* Bo's own flame is a flicker; Bo stops, rests, relights. (Expands the backlog idea above.) *"you can't pour from an empty cup."*

## Research notes

- Daily "microstressors" (2025 UK surveys): small, repeated frustrations (spills, waking late, slow walkers,
  stubbing a toe, chores undone) are what people say wear them down; mornings are the most stressful time.
  These became Season 8.

- Last-minute productivity is a measured effect: people who procrastinate more tend to become more productive as the deadline nears (deadline effect / "student syndrome").
- Roughly half of Americans call themselves people-pleasers, and about half say they often feel they can't say no.
- Perfectionism among young people has risen steadily since the late 1980s, fastest in "socially prescribed" perfectionism (feeling others expect you to be perfect).
- The "social battery" is a metaphor, not a clinical term, but it is the everyday language people use for social drain.
- Waiting on a read-but-unanswered message brings on anxiety sooner than an unread one; typing dots that appear and vanish are often described as worse than the reply itself.
- The "doorway effect" is real: walking through a doorway (an event boundary) makes people forget what they were about to do, more than walking the same distance in one room (Radvansky et al.).
- Comfort zones shrink with avoidance: the longer something is avoided, the scarier it gets, which is why the dome in Comfort Zone gets smaller.
- Sound for these shorts follows cartoon-foley practice: heightened, layered, synced to the motion; soft by design (warm low-pass on the mix, no instant attacks), with quiet ambience beds and an Animal-Crossing-style murmured voice for the captions instead of typewriter clicks.
- Hand-made feel: the motion stays smooth at 60 fps while the line "boils" (redrawn with tiny jitter about 12 times a second), blinks come at uneven gaps, and standing characters breathe.
